A level feels impossible. What am I doing wrong?

Read the small hint under the instruction. Some levels want you to act, most want you to resist. And read the instruction twice. It might be lying.

I lost my progress. Can you restore it?

Progress is stored only on your device. We never receive it, so it can't be restored after uninstalling the game or resetting progress.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Everything, including the Daily Challenge, works fully offline.

Why does the game want motion access on iPhone?

One level checks whether you shake your phone. (Spoiler: don't.) The sensor is only read during that level and the data never leaves your device.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. The game is completely free.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Reset progress in the game, or uninstall it. There is no data stored anywhere else. See the privacy policy.
